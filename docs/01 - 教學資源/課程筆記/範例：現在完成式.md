# 📖 現在完成式 Present Perfect

## 🎯 教學目標

1. 理解現在完成式的結構：have/has + p.p.
2. 區分現在完成式與過去簡單式
3. 掌握 for/since 用法

## 📖 核心內容

### 結構

| 句型 | 範例 |
|------|------|
| 肯定 | S + have/has + p.p. |
| 否定 | S + haven't/hasn't + p.p. |
| 疑問 | Have/Has + S + p.p.? |

### 三大用法

1. **經驗** — I have visited Taipei twice.
2. **持續**（for/since）— He has lived here for ten years.
3. **完成** — She has already finished her homework.

### for vs since

| for + 一段時間 | since + 時間點 |
|---|---|
| for two years | since 2020 |
| for a long time | since yesterday |
| for three hours | since 8 o'clock |
